# Xiaohongshu Cover Families

Use this reference when choosing cover styles. Combine families when useful, but keep each direction visually decisive.

## Decision Matrix

| Content / persona | Best families | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge blogger, consultant, B2B, AI/tools | Screenshot annotation, clean expert card, list/checklist, comparison |
| Personal growth, emotional story, career reflection | Portrait quote, diary collage, handwritten notes, before/after |
| Beauty, fashion, lifestyle | Product flatlay, outfit grid, portrait cutout, magazine collage |
| Food, travel, city walk | Scene hero, photo grid, map/list card, diary collage |
| Review, recommendation, shopping | Product close-up, rating card, comparison, receipt/screenshot proof |
| Tutorial, workflow, templates | Step card, screenshot annotation, checklist, split before/after |
| Strong opinion or contrarian post | Big typography, portrait reaction, red-flag list, comment-style cover |
| Case study / transformation | Before-after, metric result card, proof collage, timeline |

## Cover Families

### 1. Big Typography Promise

Use for strong hooks, opinions, listicles, growth, productivity, and AI/tool content.

- Layout: large title occupying 45-65% of cover, one strong visual anchor, small subtitle or tag.
- Visual anchor: portrait cutout, screenshot slice, product photo, or simple symbol.
- Text: result, mistake, warning, or identity line.
- Avoid: too many stickers or three competing titles.

### 2. Portrait + Quote

Use when the user's face/persona is part of trust building.

- Layout: close portrait or half-body portrait, text on clean side or translucent caption band.
- Mood: intimate, credible, direct.
- Text: one sentence that sounds spoken by the creator.
- Carousel fit: follow with proof screenshots, lessons, or story beats.

### 3. Screenshot Annotation

Use for tools, workflows, Notion/Excel/AI prompts, social media examples, data, templates.

- Layout: screenshot as main background or framed object, arrows/circles/callouts, title block.
- Mood: practical and immediately useful.
- Text: what the reader will get, plus one visible proof detail.
- Keep annotations legible; crop aggressively to the important area.

### 4. Clean Expert Card

Use for consulting, business, education, finance, law, medical-adjacent content, or high-trust topics.

- Layout: generous margins, restrained palette, strong grid, small portrait/logo optional.
- Mood: premium, calm, precise.
- Text: title + 2-3 bullet promises.
- Avoid: exaggerated claims and noisy stickers.

### 5. Magazine Collage

Use for lifestyle, fashion, beauty, travel, personal brand, creator moodboards.

- Layout: 3-6 layered photos, torn-paper or sticker rhythm, one bold title.
- Mood: curated, editorial, aspirational.
- Text: short theme line and one concrete benefit.
- Asset need: several visually consistent photos.

### 6. Product / Object Flatlay

Use for reviews, recommendations, shopping lists, beauty, desk setup, books, templates.

- Layout: product/object photo dominates; labels or mini ratings around it.
- Mood: tangible and inspectable.
- Text: recommendation angle, ranking, price/value, or use case.
- Avoid: abstract generated imagery when real product photos exist.

### 7. Before / After Split

Use for transformations, audits, makeovers, learning progress, income/workflow results.

- Layout: left-right or top-bottom split with clear labels.
- Mood: proof-driven.
- Text: the transformation headline and timeframe/context.
- Carousel fit: image 2 explains the before state, image 3-5 explain method, final summarizes.

### 8. Checklist / List Card

Use for save-worthy content: resources, steps, mistakes, templates, packing lists, reading lists.

- Layout: title at top, 4-8 list items, icons/tags, optional small image.
- Mood: useful and collectible.
- Text: concrete items, not vague categories.
- Make sure the first cover remains readable; put long details inside later carousel pages.

### 9. Comment / Chat Style

Use for relatable pain points, FAQs, myth-busting, community questions, controversial takes.

- Layout: comment bubble, chat screenshot, DM style, or question card with creator response.
- Mood: conversational and high-click.
- Text: user question or provocative statement.
- Avoid fabricating real private messages; label as reenactment if generated.

### 10. Diary / Handwritten Collage

Use for personal stories, travel, daily life, reflection, healing, creator authenticity.

- Layout: photo snapshots, handwritten-style labels, tape/sticker details, date/location stamps.
- Mood: warm, personal, less polished.
- Text: first-person hook or emotional moment.
- Use sparingly for expert accounts unless the post is explicitly personal.

## Carousel Mapping Patterns

### Tutorial

1. Cover: outcome promise or problem solved.
2. Pain: why current approach fails.
3. Overview: steps or framework.
4. Detail pages: one step per image.
5. Proof/example: screenshot or result.
6. Closing: checklist or CTA to save.

### Opinion

1. Cover: contrarian claim.
2. Context: common belief.
3. Argument 1.
4. Argument 2.
5. Example/proof.
6. Closing: nuanced takeaway.

### Review / Recommendation

1. Cover: product/category + verdict.
2. Criteria.
3. Top pick or main evidence.
4. Comparison.
5. Who should/should not use it.
6. Closing: summary rating.

### Personal Story

1. Cover: emotional hook or turning point.
2. Before state.
3. Conflict/mistake.
4. Realization.
5. Method/change.
6. After state and lesson.

### Case Study

1. Cover: result metric or before/after.
2. Starting point.
3. Constraints.
4. Strategy.
5. Execution details.
6. Outcome and replicable lesson.

## Prompt Template

Use this structure for each image-generation prompt:

```text
Create a Xiaohongshu-style carousel image, ratio [3:4/1:1/4:5].
Creator persona: [persona].
Post topic: [topic].
Image role: [cover/context/proof/step/checklist/closing].
Use assets: [file names or descriptions].
Composition: [layout family, subject placement, text zones, visual hierarchy].
Text on image: main title "[title]", subtitle "[subtitle]", labels "[labels]".
Style: [palette, typography mood, density, stickers/callouts, lighting].
Constraints: mobile-readable, coherent with the rest of the carousel, no direct copying, no fake endorsements, no distorted text, no extra limbs/faces/logos.
```
